## Why

When two different players both have units standing in the same territory,
combat does not occur between their units at all. Instead, only one
player's units are ever treated as attackers/defenders against the
territory itself — once that player's "fight" resolves, that player begins
capturing the zone uncontested, completes the capture, and then the *other*
player's units start capturing it in turn, uncontested. This repeats
indefinitely: capture ownership just alternates between the two teams on a
loop, and no actual unit-vs-unit combat between the two enemy garrisons ever
happens.

Not yet root-caused. Two candidate areas, based on reading the code so far
without live reproduction:

- `server/units.ts`'s `resolveCombatAt` only ever resolves against "the
  single defender occupying the suburb longest" (`issues/ANU-014.md`'s
  documented design choice) — with 2+ distinct enemy owners garrisoned at
  once, it's unclear whether/how combat is supposed to pick between them,
  and this may be where N-way presence collapses into only ever fighting
  one side.
- `server/territory.ts`'s `settleSuburb` capture state machine filters
  "capturers" as anyone other than the suburb's registered owner
  (`occupantOwners.filter((o) => o.ownerPlayerId !== row.ownerPlayerId)`)
  and, in the 2+-capturers branch, treats it as **contested** (freezes
  progress) rather than resolving combat between the capturers themselves
  — but the observed behavior (one side completes a capture, then the other
  starts) suggests something is letting the capturer count collapse to 1
  at a time instead of staying at 2+ for as long as both garrisons are
  actually present, which would point back to units not actually being
  destroyed/engaged between the two non-owner players.

## Done when

- Root cause identified: why doesn't `resolveCombatAt`/the arrival or
  occupancy path ever pit two non-owning players' units against each other
  when both are garrisoned in the same territory at once?
- A `spec/units.test.ts`/`spec/combat.test.ts`/`spec/capture.test.ts` case
  reproduces the loop: two players' units both present in a
  neither-owns-it (or one-owns-it) territory at the same time results in
  actual combat between them, not a silent alternation of uncontested
  captures.
- Fix lands with the reproduction test passing, and `pnpm check` green
  except the pre-existing, unrelated [[ANU-012]] map-balance failures.
