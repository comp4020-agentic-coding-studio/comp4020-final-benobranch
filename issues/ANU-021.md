## Why

The map's data-center markers (`.dc-marker--mega/major/standard` in
`server/public/map.js`/`map.css`) are currently plain solid-color SVG
circles. A free asset pack (`Free-Skill-32x32-Icons-for-Cyberpunk-Game`,
craftpix license, see its `License.txt`) ships the same icon in 14 color
variants; icon 35 reads as a small facility/server glyph. Swapping the
circles for that icon (recolored per tier) makes data centers read as
distinct map objects rather than undifferentiated colored dots, matching
the tier-marker-radius distinction (`MARKER_RADIUS`) with a visual one.

## Done when

- The three tier icons (`Skillicon<n>_35.png`, 32x32, color 9 for standard,
  color 1 for major, color 12 for mega) are copied into
  `server/public/icons/` under clear names, same 32x32 size, served by the
  existing static-file route.
- `map.js`'s data-center layer renders an `<image>` per data center at that
  icon (sized/centered on the suburb's `label_point`, replacing the
  `<circle class="dc-marker--...">` element) instead of a solid circle.
- `map.css`'s `.dc-marker--mega/major/standard` fill rules and the map
  legend's `.legend-dot--*` swatches are updated to match (legend can keep
  a colored dot or also switch to a tiny icon — just stay visually
  consistent with the new marker).
- `pnpm check` passes.
